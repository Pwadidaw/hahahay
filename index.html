Juice WRLD

A rapper and singer from Chicago who blended hip-hop, emo, and pop melody. In a short time he became one of the most influential voices of his generation, with songs that spoke openly about heartbreak, anxiety, and inner struggle.

Timeline

A short, packed career

  1. December 2, 1998

    Jarad Higgins is born in Chicago and grows up in the Homewood, Illinois area.

  2. Mid-2010s

    He starts uploading songs to SoundCloud and builds a following through early releases.

  3. 2018

    "Lucid Dreams" breaks out and Goodbye & Good Riddance is released. His name spreads worldwide.

  4. March 2019

    Death Race for Love arrives and debuts at number one on the Billboard 200.

  5. December 8, 2019

    He dies in Chicago at age 21 after suffering a seizure. The medical examiner ruled the cause an accidental drug overdose.

  6. July 2020

    Legends Never Die is released, the first album to come out after his death.

Legacy

What he left behind

Juice WRLD was known for freestyling for long stretches and writing songs fast. People who worked with him have described how he could record many songs in a single session.

The number 999 became his symbol: flipping 666 into 999 as a call to turn dark things into something better. His family carries that spirit forward through the Live Free 999 Foundation, which focuses on mental health and substance abuse.

He spoke openly about depression and anxiety in his music, and that is a big reason many listeners felt understood.

If you or someone near you is struggling, talk to someone you trust or reach out to a mental health professional.
